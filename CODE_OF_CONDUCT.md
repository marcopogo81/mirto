# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Mirto a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind and patient with people who are new, or whose English is not their first language;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — on the code, not on the person;
- accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the people who use Mirto, not only for ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit
  permission;
- posting someone else's camera images, home address, network details or any other material that
  identifies a real place or person;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

This is a project about cameras inside people's homes. Treating other people's privacy carelessly is a
conduct problem here, not only a technical one.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take appropriate and
fair corrective action in response to any behaviour deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive or
harmful.

The maintainer has the right to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate the reasons when
appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages and code review — and also when an individual is officially representing the project in public
spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub's private reporting form on this repository:
**Security → Report a vulnerability**. It is described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for security
reports, and it is used for conduct reports too, because it is the only private channel this project has
that does not require publishing someone's personal address.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obliged to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Response times follow [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md): this project is worked on at weekends, and a
first answer comes within 7 days.

**One honest limitation.** Mirto has a single maintainer. If a report concerns the maintainer, there is
no one else inside the project to send it to: raise it publicly in an issue if you are comfortable doing
so, or use GitHub's own abuse reporting, which is outside this project's control. Pretending there is an
independent committee would be worse than saying this.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer will follow these guidelines in determining the consequences of any action judged to be in
violation of this Code of Conduct.

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning, with clarity about the nature of the violation and why the
   behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour: no interaction with the people
   involved for a specified period. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the
   project for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project.
